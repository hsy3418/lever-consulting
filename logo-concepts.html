Lever Consulting — Logo Concepts

Concept 1: The Physics

Literal lever — small effort (gold dot) moves big results (green circle). Shows the core idea visually.

Concept 2: The L Launch

The L becomes a launchpad — gold arrow shooting upward. Growth through leverage.

Concept 3: Clean Wordmark

Simple and professional. The subtle tilted underline with gold fulcrum says "leverage" without being literal.

Concept 4: The Badge

Compact icon mark — works as favicon, social media profile, business card. The lever lives inside a circle.

Concept 5: Bold Dark

Modern, confident, premium feel. Gold accent bar gives the lever hint. Works on dark backgrounds.

Concept 6: The Lift

Small gold block (you) lifting the big green block (their business). The whole point in one image.
