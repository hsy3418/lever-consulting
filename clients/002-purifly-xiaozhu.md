## Problem Analysis

### 1. Positioning is unclear
She does beadwork, crochet, 法绣, and miscellaneous crafts. Account has no clear identity. Algorithm can't categorize her, viewers don't know what to expect.

### 2. Content lacks series structure
Posts jump between product types. No series, no progression, no reason to follow for "the next one."

### 3. Pricing is too low
¥88-128 for handmade work positions her as a hobbyist. Her craftsmanship supports 3-5x higher pricing.

### 4. Teaching income depends on institution
She teaches through an organization, meaning they take the larger share. She needs to build her own audience and sell direct.

### 5. English copy has errors
Several posts contain English text with grammatical issues. If targeting international market, this needs fixing.

## Strategy

### Phase 1: Focus & Position (Month 1-2)

**Concentrate on two product categories:**
1. **Necklaces/Chokers** - validated by 83 likes/71 saves on rope necklace
2. **Brooches** - validated by 92 likes/55 saves on rose brooch

Stop posting other categories for now. Not stop making them, just stop diluting the account.

**Create two content series:**
- "Purifly Necklace Collection" - 2 posts/week
- "Purifly Brooch Collection" - 1 post/week

**Content mix per week (3 posts):**
- 1x finished piece showcase (with story-driven caption)
- 1x making-of process (close-up video of beading/embroidery)
- 1x tutorial snippet (15-30 second technique, teaser for full tutorial)

### Phase 2: Pricing & Products (Month 2-3)

**Reprice everything:**
- Rope necklace: ¥388-588 (was ~¥128)
- Rose brooch: ¥488-888
- Material kit + video tutorial: ¥198-358
- Position as "手工设计师品牌" not "手工小物"

**Build material kit pipeline:**
- Each finished design becomes a material kit
- Kit = materials + printed guide + video tutorial QR code
- One design, infinite sales

### Phase 3: Independence (Month 3-6)

**Build private domain (私域):**
- Xiaohongshu posts → WeChat private traffic
- WeChat group for students/fans
- Sell courses and kits directly, no institution cut

**Course offerings:**
- Online recorded course: ¥199-399 (sell unlimited times)
- In-person small class (Shanghai): ¥800-1500/person/day, 4-6 students
- Goal: transition from institution employee to independent teacher

### Phase 4: International Expansion (When Ready)

**Requires her physical presence or strong partnership:**
- Melbourne craft markets (Finders Keepers, Big Design Market)
- Etsy store (English, global audience)
- Melbourne workshops ($180-280 AUD/person)
- Fix all English copy before launching international presence
