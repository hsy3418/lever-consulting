# Client 001: Cabinet Maker (Melbourne)

## Status: Discovery / Pre-engagement

## Overview
- **Industry:** Kitchen cabinet design and manufacturing
- **Location:** Melbourne
- **Owner:** 50+ years old, old school, no tech background
- **How we met:** Friend, teaches Heaven sailing
- **Relationship:** Friend first, client second

## Their Business
- Designs custom kitchens. Visits homes, measures, designs, then works with manufacturers to produce
- Currently uses local Australian manufacturers
- Promoted entirely by word of mouth, no online presence at all
- No website, no Facebook, no Google Business Profile

## Problems Identified
1. **No marketing or online presence.** Zero digital footprint. All business comes from word of mouth.
2. **Rising local manufacturing costs.** Australian manufacturers getting expensive, customers struggling to afford it.
3. **Exploring China imports.** Met a contact in China who knows manufacturers and shipping, but nothing confirmed yet. Lots of unknowns.
